Forgotten Lore

Dylan Weber developed this 3D environment with David Stolle, Fabian Wakenhut and Katharina Kowalczyk as a diploma project at SAE Institute Bochum, Germany.

From poem to environment

The team imagined the narrator’s study as a Victorian interior. The raven, the bust and the narrator’s chair gave the room recognizable points of reference from the poem. The project presentation traces the idea from early references through work in progress.
